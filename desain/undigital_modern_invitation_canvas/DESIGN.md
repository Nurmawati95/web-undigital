## Brand & Style

This design system drives a contemporary Indonesian digital invitation platform where celebratory culture meets clean, modern web craft. The brand balances celebratory joy with streamlined utility: couples, event hosts, and creative designers craft memorable, interactive event invitations (weddings, aqiqah, corporate launches, birthdays) shared primarily through messaging channels like WhatsApp.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Clean Minimalism** with **Vibrant Indonesian Celebratory Energy**. It rejects dated, cluttered, or overly ornate digital wedding templates in favor of structured editorial layouts, luminous white/neutral negative space, high legibility, and crisp interactive accents.

### Core Visual Tenets
- **Celebratory Clarity:** Crisp white airiness gives space to photos, typography, and guest interactions without visual noise.
- **Messaging-First Agility:** Emphasizes instant readability, high-converting WhatsApp guest RSVPs, and mobile-first gesture surfaces.
- **Polished Tactility:** Soft drop shadows paired with delicate, high-precision borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) keep interactive invitation cards feeling tailored and physical.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast readability against pristine surfaces, punctuated by energetic digital accents.

- **Primary (`#29B6F6` - Sky Cerulean):** Expresses modern optimism, airiness, and primary brand touchpoints (creation actions, progress indicators, active navigation tabs, key visual highlights).
- **Secondary (`#25D366` - Indonesian Direct Green):** The ubiquitous Indonesian communication shade (WhatsApp integration, instant confirmations, RSVP affirmations, guest book submissions, gift registry confirmations).
- **Tertiary (`#6366F1` - Royal Indigo):** Adds depth for editorial tags, VIP badges, special countdown tickers, and premium tier indicators.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Slate Navy):** Anchors all structural hierarchy and text. Deep navy-charcoal ensures pristine contrast and modern authority without the harshness of pure black.

### Surface Hierarchy
- `surface-canvas`: `#FAFAFC` (subtle warm-neutral canvas that prevents screen glare).
- `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` (pure elevated card backgrounds).
- `surface-subtle`: `#F1F5F9` (pill fills, input backgrounds, inert zones).
- `border-crisp`: `#E2E8F0` (ultra-sharp structural outlines).

## Typography

The platform deploys **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles. Its geometric construction, humanized apertures, and authentic Indonesian design pedigree make it uniquely fitting for a premier local digital invitation experience.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set tight with subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700` and `800`) to present event titles, names of bride and groom, dates, and campaign hooks with crisp editorial impact.
- **Body:** Retains comfortable line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) for multi-line Indonesian texts, cultural blessings (doa/ayat), itinerary details, and venue directions.
- **Labels & Micro-UI:** Leverages medium and semi-bold weights with slight positive tracking to ensure instant scanability on mobile screens under outdoor Indonesian sunlight.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system pairs an adaptive 12-column desktop studio grid with an ergonomic single-column mobile viewport (max-width `460px` for mobile invitation preview frames).

### Grid Adaptation
- **Mobile (`<640px`):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem (16px)`, `gutter: 1rem (16px)`. Modals and invitation views stick to full-width or bottom-sheet modes.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`):** 8 columns, `margin-md: 2rem (32px)`, `gutter-md: 1.5rem (24px)`. Split view for template picker and live preview.
- **Desktop (`>1024px`):** 12 columns, `margin-lg: 3.5rem (56px)`, `gutter-lg: 2rem (32px)`. Central or dual-pane workspace: left controls palette (`420px`), right dynamic mobile canvas simulator (`fixed`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through ultra-soft ambient drop shadows combined with hairline crisp outlines (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). This avoids heavy skeuomorphic shading while preventing elements from washing out on pure white canvases.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Baseline neutral canvas background `#FAFAFC`. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Panels):** Subtle soft lift with border containment:
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);`
  - `border: 1px solid #E2E8F0;`
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Controls):** Crisp engagement response:
  - `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(41, 182, 246, 0.12), 0 2px 8px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
  - `border: 1px solid #BAE6FD;`
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Action Bars):** High structural elevation:
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 35px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 6px 12px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
  - `border: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8);`
  - `backdrop-filter: blur(12px);`

## Shapes

The design system adopts a modern curved visual vocabulary. Standard card modules, template containers, and sheet overlays specify `rounded-2xl` (`1rem / 16px`) to project approachability and modern softness.

- **Containers & Cards:** `16px` (`rounded-2xl`) for invitation preview sheets, RSVP forms, gift registry blocks, and event timelines.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons):** `12px` (`rounded-xl`) to balance internal corner nesting within parent `16px` containers.
- **Tags, Chips & Badges:** `9999px` (fully pill-shaped) for operational states (`Konfirmasi Hadir`, `Akad`, `Resepsi`, `Exclusive Template`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Brand):** Sky blue fill (`#29B6F6`), white bold text, `12px` radius. On hover: shifts to `#0288D1` with subtle cyan glow (`0 4px 14px rgba(41, 182, 246, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Affirmative (WhatsApp RSVP):** Emerald fill (`#25D366`), white bold text, integrated WhatsApp SVG icon on left. Hover: `#1EBE5D` with emerald glow (`0 4px 14px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.35)`).
- **Outline / Secondary:** Pure white card surface, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: border `#29B6F6`, text `#0288D1`.

### Cards & Invitation Modules
- Built with `rounded-2xl` (`16px`), pure white surface, and Level 1 elevation.
- Always bounded by crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
- Header cards feature full-bleed imagery rounded at top corners with optional gradient scrims for date/name overlays.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Surface fill: `#F8FAFC`.
- Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Corner radius: `12px`.
- Typography: `15px` Plus Jakarta Sans.
- Focus state: Surface turns pure `#FFFFFF`, border sharpens to `2px solid #29B6F6`, paired with `0 0 0 3px rgba(41, 182, 246, 0.15)` ring.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Height: `28px` to `32px`, pill radius (`9999px`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **WhatsApp RSVP Positive:** Light mint background (`#DCFCE7`), text `#15803D`.
- **Active Feature / Sky:** Light cyan background (`#E0F2FE`), text `#0369A1`.
- **Event Category Tag:** Neutral background (`#F1F5F9`), text `#334155`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: `20px x 20px`. Radius: `6px` for checkboxes, circular for radios.
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: `#29B6F6` background, crisp white checkmark/dot icon, zero blur.

### Specialized Indonesian Invitation Modules
- **Angpao Digital / Gift Registry Card:** Card with bank/e-wallet logos (BCA, Mandiri, GoPay), copyable account number button with instant toast feedback, and crisp QRIS preview frame.
- **Audio Autoplay Floating Toggle:** Floating circular pill button (`44px x 44px`) fixed at bottom-right of preview screens, translucent white glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) with animated vinyl disk or soundwave indicator in Sky Blue.
- **RSVP Guest Attendance Switcher:** 3-tier segmented pill control: "Hadir" (Emerald tint), "Ragu-ragu" (Amber tint), "Tidak Hadir" (Rose tint).
